The Science

How Dry Extraction Works in 4 Simple Steps

Why pump hundreds of gallons into your living room when crystalline molecular extraction does the job better?

01

Bio-Mist Pre-Conditioning

A gentle citrus atomized mist breaks the electrostatic bond holding greasy dirt and grime to carpet fibers without soaking into backing.

02

Micro-Sponge Compound

Millions of organic, biodegradable micro-sponges are dispersed across the carpet. Each sponge absorbs suspended soil and dissolves spots.

03

Counter-Rotating Brushes

Dual cylindrical brushes spin in opposite directions, lifting the carpet pile 360 degrees and working the micro-sponges down to the root.

04

High-Velocity HEPA Extraction

Industrial HEPA filtration vacuums extract 100% of the soil-filled micro-sponges. Carpet is fluffed, allergen-free, and bone dry in < 10 mins.

Everything you need to know about our revolutionary 10-minute dry extraction technology.

Quick Dry Carpets uses high-tech micro-encapsulation: organic microscopic sponges and bio-enzymes absorb dissolved soil at the molecular level. Counter-rotating brushes lift pile, and our HEPA vacuum extracts the soil-filled crystals. Because we use 95% less moisture, fibers dry within minutes, while cleaning 3x deeper.

Wick-back occurs when steam cleaning soaks moisture into the carpet padding. As that water evaporates over 24–48 hours, it pulls old subsurface soil up to the fiber tips. Because our dry technology never wets the padding, wicking is physically impossible. Once a stain is gone, it stays gone.
